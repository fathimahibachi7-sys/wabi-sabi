It Ends with Us

Colleen Hoover

01 It Ends
with Us Colleen Hoover

On breaking cycles, boundaries and difficult choices.

What stayed with me

Some books stay with you because of their story, while others stay because of a few ideas that you keep thinking about long after you have finished reading. It Ends with Us was that kind of book for me.

What stayed with me most was the idea that cycles can be extremely difficult to break. It made me think about how hard it can be to walk away from something familiar, even when we know that it is hurting us.

Another thought that stayed with me was the decision to stop a painful pattern instead of carrying it forward. To me, that represents choosing yourself and having the courage to make a difficult decision when it is necessary.

I also liked the feeling behind reaching the shore. After everything the characters go through, it felt like finally reaching a place where there can be peace, clarity and a new beginning.

What I appreciated about the book is that it did not leave me thinking only about the romance. It made me think about choices, boundaries and the courage it takes to break a cycle.

For me, the book stayed with me because sometimes the hardest ending can also be the beginning of something healthier.
