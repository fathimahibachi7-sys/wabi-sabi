Book 04

Ikigai

Héctor García & Francesc Miralles

04 Ikigai Héctor García & Francesc Miralles

On purpose, simple living and meaningful everyday life.

What stayed with me

Ikigai was a book that made me look at happiness and a meaningful life in a different way. What stayed with me most was the book's discussion of people in Japan who live long, active and fulfilling lives.

I was especially interested in the idea that a long life is not only about living longer, but also about living with a sense of purpose. The concept of ikigai, often described as a reason to get up in the morning, made me think about how important it is to have something that gives meaning to our everyday lives.

I also liked the way the book talks about simple habits and lifestyle choices. Staying active, eating mindfully, spending time with other people, maintaining friendships and continuing to do things that bring purpose can all make everyday life more meaningful.

Another thing I took from the book was the importance of not completely stopping just because we get older. Having something to do, people to share life with and a reason to keep going can make everyday life feel more purposeful.

The book also made me appreciate the value of slowing down and enjoying ordinary moments instead of always looking for something bigger. Happiness does not necessarily have to come from a complicated or luxurious life.

For me, Ikigai was less about finding one perfect purpose and more about understanding that a meaningful life can be built through the way we live every day. It left me thinking about what my own reason for getting up every morning could be.

Sometimes a meaningful life is not built from huge moments, but from small things we continue to care about every day.
